Web Story for Makers

How does a website actually get made? Four stories, one hub

Early websites were simple pages full of plain text.
Over time, people turned wishes like "I want it to look better," "I want it to move with the mouse," and "I want to build it faster" into technology one by one, shaping the web pages we use every day.

Building the web splits into four tracks: Structure (how to lay out text and images), Style (how to make it look good), Motion (how to make the screen respond), and Tools (what tools make building easier).
Each story follows what people found frustrating and how they solved it.

Start by skimming the big flow, then jump into whichever track catches your eye.
Don't worry if unfamiliar terms appear—each story explains what people of that era were trying to fix.
